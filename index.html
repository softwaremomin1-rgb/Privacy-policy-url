Privacy Policy

Islamic Companion

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Developer/Owner: Tabish

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Islamic Companion handles information when you use the app. The current version is primarily an Islamic reference and utility application providing Quran content, translations, Hadith content, prayer times, Quran audio, bookmarks, and related features.

2. Information We Collect

Islamic Companion does not require you to create an account and, in the current version, does not ask you to submit your name, email address, phone number, password, or payment information inside the app.

Bookmarks and similar preferences are stored locally on your device using browser/app local storage. They are not intentionally uploaded to our servers.

3. Location Information

The app can use your device's location when you choose the location-based prayer-time feature. Location is used to obtain prayer times for your current coordinates. The app also allows prayer times to be requested by entering a city manually, so location access is not required for that method.

The app does not intentionally store your GPS coordinates on a personal account.

4. Camera and Microphone Permissions

The Android application package includes camera and microphone permissions because of its WebView/platform configuration. The current app interface does not use camera or microphone recording as a core feature, and we do not intentionally collect, store, or sell camera images, videos, microphone recordings, or audio recordings.

5. Third-Party Services

To provide content and functionality, the app may request information from third-party services, including:

  • AlAdhan API — prayer-time data.
  • AlQuran Cloud API — Quran text and translations.
  • Islamic Network CDN — Quran audio.
  • Hadith API/CDN — Hadith content.
  • Google Fonts — fonts used by the interface.

These services may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of normal internet communication. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply.

6. Internet and Security

The app uses an internet connection to retrieve Quran, Hadith, prayer-time, audio, and related content. We take reasonable steps to avoid intentionally collecting unnecessary personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Islamic Companion is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

8. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. We do not intentionally share personal information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current app does not require a user account, we do not maintain a personal account database for users. Locally stored bookmarks/preferences can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same privacy-policy page with a revised effective date.
